About this collection

The Balanced Tadka shares Indian and Indian-inspired cooking adaptations for readers interested in blood glucose awareness, higher-protein meals, vegetables, fiber, and practical meal prep. We credit cookbook authors and online creators when a recipe is based on their work, and we explain what was changed and why. Some recipes are independently developed and are labeled accordingly. Recipes still in development are excluded from the public recipe library.

Medical and nutrition disclaimer

This website is for general education and cooking inspiration only; it does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or individualized nutrition advice. “Diabetes friendly,” “higher protein,” and “weight-management friendly” describe recipe intentions, not guaranteed outcomes. Blood glucose responses vary by individual, portion, medication, and other factors. Nutrition estimates, where supplied, are approximate and are not laboratory verified. People with diabetes, pregnant people, and children may have different dietary needs. Consult an appropriate qualified clinician or registered dietitian.

Recipe credit policy

Each adaptation identifies the original creator and source when known, explains the changes, and uses newly written directions. Where original recipe details cannot be verified, entries are marked source verification needed and should not be publicly published as fully attributed adaptations until verified. Attribution is not a substitute for permission to reproduce copyrighted text or photographs.
