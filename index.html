Welcome to my kitchen

The Balanced Tadka

Indian flavors, thoughtfully reimagined.

I’m a busy working mom with an Indian background, always looking for easy, higher-protein, lower-carb ways to make my family’s favorite dishes. This website is my way of sharing some of the recipes I’ve curated along the way.

I believe cooking is an art, not a science. So play with the ingredients, change things to suit your tastes, and get creative. If there’s one thing I know, it’s that your family will love you and your food—always.

I believe that even the simplest meal cooked at home can be better than eating out. Cooking at home gives you the freedom to choose your ingredients, decide what goes into your food, and nourish your family in a way that works for you. It doesn’t have to be complicated or perfect—sometimes the simplest homemade meals are the best.

A little about me

A busy mom, an Indian kitchen, and lots of experimenting.

Between work and family life, I’m always looking for practical ways to bring more protein and vegetables—and fewer refined carbohydrates—into the dishes we already love. Some recipes are rooted in Indian home cooking; others are everyday vegetarian family favorites.

I share these recipes as starting points, not strict rules. Taste as you go, use what you have, and make each dish your own. Cooking should be creative, flexible, and fun.

When a recipe is inspired by another cook, I credit the source and explain my changes. Some recipes are still being refined in my kitchen.
